art-fill is free to download. Four sample paintings — Girl with a Pearl Earring, The Starry Night, The Scream and Mona Lisa — can be painted start to finish and saved to Photos without paying anything.

The Full Version is a one-time in-app purchase. It adds your own photos, as many as you like, and the other eight sample paintings. It is not a subscription: it is paid for once, does not renew, and is never charged again.

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. You can ask for one at reportaproblem.apple.com. If a refund is given, the Full Version locks again. Paintings you have already made from your own photos stay on your iPad.
