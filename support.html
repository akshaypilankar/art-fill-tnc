art-fill — Support

art-fill is a calm way to paint on iPad, at any age. A photo of someone or somewhere you love becomes a numbered canvas you fill in yourself, one colour at a time.

Something wrong, or something you want? Email ccloudcreativez3@gmail.com. It is one person, so a reply may take a day or two, but every message is read.

When reporting a problem, these three things make it fixable: your iPad model, your iPadOS version (Settings → General → About), and what you did just before it went wrong.

Getting started

Starting free. The Starry Night, Mona Lisa, The Scream and Girl with a Pearl Earring are on the first screen and free to paint, start to finish. Tap one to begin.

Making a canvas from your own photo (full version). Tap Choose Photo and pick any picture. Converting it takes a few seconds. Pick Relaxing, Balanced or Detailed first — that sets how many areas the picture is broken into. Relaxing gives fewer, larger areas; Detailed gives more colours and finer work.

Painting. Pick a colour from the numbered swatches along the bottom, then a tool from the strip on the left:

  • Fill — the top button. Tap an area to fill the whole of it.
  • Brush — drag on the canvas to paint by hand.
  • Eraser — drag to lift paint off again.

The brush and the eraser share a width slider, which appears under them while either is selected.

Getting around. Two fingers to pan and pinch to zoom. Two-finger tap to undo, or use the undo and redo buttons.

Apple Pencil

Press harder for a wider stroke; lift off gradually for a taper. You can rest your hand on the screen while you draw — a resting palm is ignored while the Pencil is drawing.

Free and full version

What is free?
Four paintings — The Starry Night, Mona Lisa, The Scream and Girl with a Pearl Earring. Paint them start to finish and save them to Photos; nothing in them is held back.

What does the full version add?
Your own photos, as many as you like, and the other eight classic paintings — the ones with a padlock. It is one purchase: no subscription, no ads, nothing else to buy.

I bought the full version, but I still see padlocks.
On a new iPad, or after reinstalling, tap any padlocked painting, then Restore Purchase at the top of the sheet. Use the same Apple Account the full version was bought with.

My child asked to buy it, and nothing has opened yet.
With Ask to Buy, the purchase waits for a parent to approve it. Once it is approved, everything opens on its own — there is nothing to tap again.

Common questions

Which detail setting should I pick?
Balanced suits most photos. Detailed is best for photos of people — faces keep their eyes, brows and smiles there — and Relaxing gives a quicker canvas of larger, simpler areas. When you choose a photo, the app suggests a setting if it thinks another would suit it better.

My photo came out soft.
A small photo — often a copy saved from a message or a web page — is enlarged before it is converted. That keeps shapes and faces intact, but it cannot add detail the photo never had. The full-size original from your camera works best.

Why won't a colour go where I want it?
Stay in the lines is probably on: each colour then only paints the areas its number calls for. Turn it off in ⋯ → Stay in the lines to paint any colour anywhere.

How do I see what it is supposed to look like?
The eye button, at the bottom of the strip on the left. It shows the finished design, and switches back leaving your painting exactly as it was — it never fills anything in for you.

The numbers are in the way.
⋯ → Show numbers turns them off. They also fade out on their own as paint covers them.

Where did my painting go?
Paintings save themselves when you leave the canvas, and appear under Your paintings on the first screen. There is no save button and nothing to remember.

How do I keep a finished painting?
⋯ → Save to Photos writes it to your photo library at full resolution, without the numbers or outlines.

How do I rename or delete a painting?
In the painting, ⋯ → Rename. On the first screen, touch and hold a painting for Rename and Delete.

Can I start over?
⋯ → Reset painting clears the canvas. It cannot be undone, so it asks first.

Does it work offline?
Painting always does. Only buying or restoring the full version needs a connection, because that goes through the App Store.

Requirements

iPad running iPadOS 17 or later. Apple Pencil is supported but not required — a finger works everywhere a Pencil does.

Privacy

art-fill collects nothing. Your photos stay on your iPad. Full policy: Privacy Policy.
